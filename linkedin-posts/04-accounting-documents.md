# LinkedIn Post 4 - Accounting (document drudgery & advisory shift)

---

We'll say it plainly: nobody hired qualified accountants so they could retype invoices.

And yet, in most finance teams, a real slice of the week goes exactly there. Reading PDFs, keying line items, fixing the transposed digit someone caught three days later. People who trained for years to interpret a business, spending their afternoons assembling the raw data instead.

Document AI has quietly crossed the line from "promising" to "actually reliable" on this. Biloop reads the invoice the moment it lands, pulls every field, checks it against the PO, and only flags a human when something genuinely doesn't reconcile. Something like four out of five invoices go through with nobody typing a thing.

For a practice, the arithmetic is hard to argue with. Same headcount, more clients, and the hours that open up go to advisory work clients will actually pay a premium for.

The firms we'd bet on over the next few years aren't the ones with the biggest data-entry teams. They're the ones quietly dismantling that work and moving up the value chain.
