# LinkedIn Post 3 — Accounting (the month-end close)

---

📊 Ask a finance team what month-end feels like, and you'll hear the same word: marathon.

Five to eight days of reconciling, chasing, and keying numbers from PDFs — just to tell leadership what happened a month ago.

By the time the numbers land, the month they describe is already history. If there's a problem, you're reacting to the past.

It doesn't have to work this way.

Biloop runs reconciliation continuously in the background — matching bank lines, payables and receivables every day, not in one painful batch. Clean matches post automatically. Only true exceptions reach a human.

The books are effectively always close-ready. Teams move from a 5–8 day close to 1–2 days — or to a genuinely continuous close.

That's the real shift AI brings to accounting: finance stops *recording* the past and starts *steering* the future.
