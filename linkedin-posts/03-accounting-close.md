# LinkedIn Post 3 - Accounting (the month-end close)

---

A controller told me last week that her team hasn't had a normal first week of the month in four years. Every single one disappears into close. Reconcile, chase, key numbers off PDFs, then present figures that describe a month that's already over.

I keep turning that over in my head. We've automated most of the business by now, and the one function whose job is to tell leadership where the money is still runs on a five to eight day manual sprint, every month, forever.

The fix isn't more people or a longer weekend. It's honestly a bit boring: let reconciliation run quietly every day instead of saving it all for month-end. Bank lines match to the ledger as they arrive, the clean ones post themselves, and a person only looks at the real exceptions. Do that and "closing the books" stops being an event. The numbers are basically always current.

We build this into Biloop, but the principle matters more than the tool. If your close still eats a week, it's worth asking what that week is actually buying you, because it usually isn't insight.
